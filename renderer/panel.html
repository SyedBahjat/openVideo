Sharing

Share links save the video to your own GitHub account: free, and it stays yours. The repo is public, so anyone who finds it can watch. Don’t share anything private.

  1. Connect GitHub. Your browser opens GitHub with a code; check it matches the one shown here, then click Authorize.

  2. Click Share on a recording. The first share creates openvideo-recordings in your GitHub and copies the link.
